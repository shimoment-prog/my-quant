The following communication was made available to employees of Marvell via email by Matt Murphy, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Marvell Technology, Inc. (“Marvell”) on November 10, 2026.

From: Matt Murphy

To: GR-Marvell-All

Subject: Putting the Week in Perspective

Date: 11/10/2026

Team:

Last week was an important one for Marvell. We announced our agreement to combine with Lumentum, bringing together two industry leaders and positioning Marvell to become the category leader in AI connectivity. Following the announcement, both companies held All-Hands meetings to discuss the combination and answer questions. Michael Hurlston and I also appeared together on CNBC’s Squawk on the Street and Liz Claman’s The Claman Countdown on Fox Business. Throughout the week, leaders from both companies connected with analysts, investors and key customers. Michael and I also traveled to Boston and New York with our respective Investor Relations teams to meet with some of our largest shareholders. I wanted to share a few thoughts from those conversations, put the recent volatility in our stock into perspective, and recognize the tremendous work that went into reaching this milestone.

While some of the initial analyst commentary and media coverage reflected a misunderstanding of the strategic rationale for the combination, our conversations with major shareholders told a very different story. They understood the opportunity to bring together leaders in silicon and optics, and the potential to create significant long-term value. They also recognized Marvell’s track record of successful acquisitions and the role those transactions have played in building the company we are today. Many gave us credit for making a bold strategic move at a time when both companies are experiencing strong growth.

That doesn’t mean there weren’t questions. One we heard frequently was, “Given the strong growth both companies are experiencing, why now?” The answer goes back to the connectivity-centric strategy we outlined at our Investor Day last month and the importance of anticipating major technology transitions. When we acquired Inphi, we positioned Marvell ahead of the transition to scale-out optical connectivity. Today, we’re approaching another major architectural shift as optical connectivity moves into scale-up and scale-in applications. Customers are making important technology decisions now that will shape the next generation of AI infrastructure, and we believe this is the right time to bring these capabilities together.

Putting the recent market volatility in perspective

Over the past month, we’ve seen substantial selling across the broader AI market, including AI connectivity stocks specifically. Part of what’s driving this is uncertainty about how long the current AI infrastructure investment cycle will continue. The semiconductor and optical industries have historically been cyclical, and investors are trying to anticipate where we are in that cycle. Questions about the pace and sustainability of AI infrastructure spending have contributed to significant volatility across the sector. The recent U.S. midterm elections also brought increased attention to the rapid expansion of data centers and the possibility of additional restrictions on their development.

The charts below help put the recent decline into perspective. They compare the performance of Marvell and Lumentum with broader groups of AI and connectivity companies, as well as the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) and S&P 500, over the past month and year to date.

 

 

The decline over the past month has been significant, particularly across AI connectivity companies. Marvell shares declined approximately 51%, closely in line with the 49% average decline among our connectivity peers. Lumentum experienced a similar decline. These comparisons illustrate just how widespread the recent selloff has been, particularly among companies focused on AI connectivity.

It’s also important to keep the longer-term picture in mind. Despite the recent pullback, Marvell shares are still up approximately 66% year to date, outperforming both the connectivity and broader AI peer groups. More importantly, the long-term trends driving investment in AI infrastructure remain strong, as we discussed at Investor Day last month. Stock prices will fluctuate, but what we can control is how well we execute our strategy, deliver for our customers and build long-term value.

Recognizing the team behind the announcement

I also want to recognize the tremendous work that went into last week’s announcement. Combinations of this magnitude are incredibly complex, requiring months of intensive work across multiple teams throughout the company. From due diligence and financial analysis to negotiating the agreement, navigating legal and regulatory requirements, and preparing for the announcement itself, an enormous amount of work happens behind the scenes, often under tremendous time pressure and with little room for error.

I’ve had the privilege of seeing our teams come together on these projects many times over the years, and I’m continually impressed by the professionalism, judgment and commitment they bring to the work. This announcement was another example of just how good our teams are at what they do. I’m incredibly proud of everyone who contributed and grateful for the extraordinary effort that went into reaching this important milestone.

As I mentioned during last week’s investor call, simply acquiring a company doesn’t create value. It’s what we do afterward — how we bring teams and technologies together, invest in innovation and execute for our customers — that ultimately determines our success. We’ve demonstrated that ability repeatedly over the past decade, and we have a great deal of work ahead of us as we prepare for this combination.

The transaction isn’t expected to close until the first half of calendar 2028, and until then, Marvell and Lumentum will continue operating as independent companies. Our focus remains on delivering against our commitments, supporting our customers and executing on the significant opportunities already in front of us.

Thank you for everything you’re doing during this important time for Marvell.

Matt
