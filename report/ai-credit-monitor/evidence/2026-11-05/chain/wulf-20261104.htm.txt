Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. Justified Data Campus Credit Support Agreements As previously disclosed by TeraWulf Inc. (“TeraWulf” or the “Company”), in July 2026, the Company’s subsidiary Raylan Data LLC (“Raylan”) entered into a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic, PBC (“Anthropic”), as tenant, to provide Anthropic with approximately 401 MW of critical IT load for high-performance computing (“HPC”) operations at the Company’s data center campus located in Hawesville, Kentucky. Anthropic’s obligation to pay rent commences upon the delivery of each data hall at the applicable leased premises, which consist of CB-1, CB-2 and CB-3 (the leases for such premises are referred to individually as a “Justified Lease” and, collectively, as the “Justified Leases”). Delivery of the capacity under the Justified Leases is expected to occur in phases beginning in late 2027 and concluding in the second quarter of 2028. In connection with the Justified Leases, on November 4, 2026, the Company entered into three Credit Support Agreements for CB-1, CB-2 and CB-3, respectively (each a “Google Credit Support Agreement” and collectively, the “Google Credit Support Agreements”), with Raylan, Justified DataPower LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, Anthropic and Google LLC (“Google”), pursuant to which Google agreed to backstop (the “Justified Google Backstop”) certain obligations of Anthropic under the Justified Leases. The Justified Google Backstop under each Google Credit Support Agreement will become effective as of the commencement date for the final data hall under the corresponding Justified Lease and will terminate upon the conditions described in the Google Credit Support Agreements, including Anthropic (or its affiliate, if such affiliate has agreed to guarantee Anthropic’s obligations under the applicable Justified Lease) achieving and maintaining certain investment grade credit ratings for a period of at least three months. The Justified Google Backstop provides that in the event of a payment default under a Justified Lease, or if Anthropic becomes subject to an insolvency event, following notice from Raylan, Google will have the option to (i) pay all rent currently due under the applicable Justified Lease and assume the Justified Lease as the tenant thereunder, or (ii) under certain circumstances, pay a certain termination fee pursuant to the applicable Google Credit Support Agreement; provided that upon payment of such termination fee, Google would be entitled to acquire an equity interest in Raylan. In addition, in the event of a payment default under a Justified Lease, Google may defer its election of the foregoing options (i) and (ii) for a certain specified period, during which deferral period Google will pay all unpaid rent under the applicable Justified Lease and all additional rent that becomes due. The termination fee payable under the Justified Google Backstop amortizes over a defined term following the anticipated effectiveness of the corresponding Google Credit Support Agreement. The description of the Google Credit Support Agreements is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full and complete terms of the Google Credit Support Agreements, a form of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
