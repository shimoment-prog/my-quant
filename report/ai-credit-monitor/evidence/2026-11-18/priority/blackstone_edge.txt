Blackstone REIT to buy majority stake in US$5b Amazon data centre portfolio Wednesday 18 Nov 2026 BURSA SGX Home Tech Make The Edge Malaysia your preferred source on Google (Nov 16): Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust said on Monday it had agreed to buy a 75.1% controlling stake in two joint ventures with Amazon Web Services, in a deal valuing the data centre portfolio at about US$5 billion (RM20.5 billion).
The deal marks the first investment by the recently listed real estate investment trust, which focuses on owning newly constructed data centers leased to large cloud computing companies on long-term contracts.
The two joint ventures will own 13 newly constructed powered shells across two AWS data centre campuses, with Amazon affiliates retaining a 24.9% stake.
AWS will lease back the entire portfolio on a triple net basis with an initial lease term of 12 years and a fully extended lease term of 32 years, the company said.
Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust said it expects to use US$1.9 billion of cash raised through its initial public offering to fund the acquisition
The remaining funding, including transaction costs, is expected to come from about US$2.5 billion in non-recourse mortgage loans secured by the properties.
The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.
Morgan Stanley, CBRE Capital Markets, Citi, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan and Wells Fargo Bank are serving as financial advisers on the transaction
