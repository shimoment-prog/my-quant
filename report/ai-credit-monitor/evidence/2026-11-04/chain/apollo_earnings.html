For the Principal Investing segment, PII is the sum of (i) realized performance fees, including certain realizations received in the form of equity and capital solutions fees and other, net earned above certain performance thresholds, and (ii) realized investment income, less (x) realized principal investing compensation expense, excluding expense related to equity-based compensation, and (y) certain corporate compensation and non-compensation expenses. • “Adjusted Net Income” or “ANI” represents Segment Income less HoldCo interest and other financing costs and estimated income taxes.
