 EX-99.1 2 erex9913q2026.htm EX-99.1 Document Apollo Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results New York, November 3, 2026 — Apollo Global Management, Inc. (NYSE: APO) (together with its consolidated subsidiaries, “Apollo”) today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Marc Rowan, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer at Apollo said, “Our strong third quarter results reflect the flywheel of our model, which starts with originating increasing amounts of good assets. Origination reached a new high of $114 billion in the quarter, and over the last twelve months, activity exceeded $350 billion while our clients entrusted us with $270 billion of inflows. Behind record pipelines and tangible momentum across the business, our core earnings power is growing and poised for a robust close to 2026 with a strong setup for 2027. Our mission remains clear: we are continuing to build a better, faster, stronger Apollo that is well positioned to lead the next generation of financial services.” Apollo issued a full detailed presentation of its third quarter ended September 30, 2026 results, which can be viewed on Apollo’s Investor Relations website at ir.apollo.com . Dividend Apollo Global Management, Inc. has declared a cash dividend of $0.5625 per share of its Common Stock for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. This dividend will be paid on November 30, 2026 to holders of record at the close of business on November 18, 2026. The declaration and payment of dividends on the Common Stock are at the sole discretion of Apollo Global Management, Inc.’s board of directors. Apollo cannot assure its stockholders that they will receive any dividends in the future. Conference Call Apollo will host a public audio webcast on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time. During the webcast, members of Apollo’s senior management team will review Apollo’s financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The webcast may be accessed at ir.apollo.com . For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, there will be a replay of the webcast available at the same link one hour after the event. Apollo distributes its earnings releases via its website and email distribution lists. Those interested in receiving firm updates by email can sign up for them at ir.apollo.com . About Apollo Apollo is a high-growth, global alternative asset manager. In our asset management business, we seek to provide our clients excess return at every point along the risk-reward spectrum from investment grade credit to private equity. For more than three decades, our investing expertise across our fully integrated platform has served the financial return needs of our clients and provided businesses with innovative capital solutions for growth. Through Athene, our retirement services business, we specialize in helping clients achieve financial security by providing a suite of retirement savings products and acting as a solutions provider to institutions. Our patient, creative, and knowledgeable approach to investing aligns our clients, businesses we invest in, our employees, and the communities we impact, to expand opportunity and achieve positive outcomes. As of September 30, 2026, Apollo had approximately $1.07 trillion of assets under management.
