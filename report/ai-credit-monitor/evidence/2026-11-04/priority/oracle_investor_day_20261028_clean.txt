At the event co-CEO Clay Magouryk, co-CEO Mike Sicilia and CFO Hilary Maxson presented and responded to questions about the company’s strategy and updated medium-term financial guidance. At its Investor Day, Oracle described how its unique portfolio positioning across cloud infrastructure, database and applications is expected to drive total revenue growth of more than 35% CAGR from FY26 through FY30, reaching total revenue of $225 billion or more in FY30. It also outlined its expectations for strong profitability and free cash flow generation from each of its three business models. Following a period of peak capex in FY27-FY28, capital expenditures are expected to step-down in FY29, supporting positive free cash flow of at least $30 billion in FY29. Oracle committed to limiting additional funding to be raised in the capital markets for its FY27-FY30 investment cycle to $20 billion, currently expected to be $10 billion in equity and $10 billion in gross debt. This incremental funding requirement excludes refinancing of existing debt. This is in addition to the $20 billion at-the-market equity raise completed in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. Vendor financing, customer-provided hardware and customer prepayments are expected to reduce upfront cash requirements through the transition to positive free cash flow. Oracle reaffirmed its fiscal 2027 outlook for total revenue of at least $90 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share of $8.10. The company’s capital allocation priorities remain maintaining investment-grade credit ratings, paying a dividend and funding growth opportunities.
