The acquisition brings together leading AI model researchers and AMD engineers to shape the next generation of AMD hardware, software and systems for the open AI ecosystem. “Delivering the infrastructure that will power the next era of super intelligence requires us to optimize compute platforms and models together,” said Dr. Lisa Su, chair and CEO of AMD. “We are thrilled to welcome Fei-Fei Li and the exceptional World Labs team to AMD. Their pioneering model research will help shape our future hardware, software and systems. Together, we will enable researchers and developers across the ecosystem to push the boundaries of what AI can do.” World Labs develops AI models that create and simulate interactive 3D environments, as well as technology for robotic learning and simulation. Dr. Fei-Fei Li joins AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting directly to Su. The World Labs team will continue advancing foundational model research while collaborating with AMD engineers to apply insights from emerging model capabilities to the development of future AI infrastructure for customers and developers across the open AI ecosystem.
