Item 8.01 Other Information
On November 16, 2026, Ciena Corporation (“Ciena”) announced the appointment of Greg Friesen as Executive Vice President and Chief Product Officer, effective as of such date. In this role, Mr. Friesen will have responsibility for product line management, solutions engineering and introduction, and quality, and will work closely with Ciena’s research and development organization to advance product strategy. In connection and contemporaneous with this appointment, Brodie Gage will be leaving Ciena and will no longer serve in the role as Ciena’s Executive Vice President and Chief Product and Technology Officer.
