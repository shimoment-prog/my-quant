Revenue Revenue for the third quarter was a record $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago and up 20% sequentially. Data Center revenue for the third quarter was a record $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago and up 21% sequentially. Our Hyperscale and AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise platforms both grew substantially, driven by the introduction of our Rubin infrastructure, our fastest architecture ramp ever, standalone Vera CPUs and the continued growth of Blackwell Ultra. Shipments of Data Center Hopper products to China during the quarter were $2.4 billion. Edge Computing revenue for the third quarter was $8.2 billion, up 42% from a year ago and up 14% sequentially. Strong Blackwell workstation sales and edge compute for Physical AI applications drove the increases, while higher memory and system prices continue to affect consumer PC demand. Gross Margin GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins for the third quarter increased from a year ago on improved mix from Blackwell Ultra. GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins decreased slightly sequentially, reflecting the initial Rubin ramp. Expenses GAAP operating expenses for the third quarter were up 57% from a year ago and up 9% sequentially, and non-GAAP operating expenses were up 52% from a year ago and up 7% sequentially. These increases were driven by higher compute infrastructure and employee compensation and benefits costs. Other Income, Net and Income Tax GAAP other income, net includes equity securities gains or losses, interest income, interest expense, and other income and expense. Non-GAAP other income, net excludes equity securities gains or losses and certain other income and expense. Net gains from equity securities for the third quarter were $1.8 billion, comprised of net gains of $2.8 billion from our publicly held equity securities and a net loss of $1.0 billion from our non-marketable equity securities, including impairment of $1.8 billion. GAAP effective tax rate for the third quarter was 17.4%, an increase from a year ago, primarily due to higher revenue. Non-GAAP effective tax rate for the third quarter was 17.0%. Commitments We have entered into strategic commitments across our supply, infrastructure, and partner ecosystems to capitalize on future growth opportunities and support our business. We’ve partnered with our extensive network of suppliers to secure the critical components needed to meet demand for the next several years. Our supply commitments increased from $279 billion last quarter to $390 billion, primarily related to the multi-year procurement of memory supply. Our cloud service agreements and data center lease commitments together of $102 billion, an increase from last quarter’s $74 billion, provide the physical and cloud infrastructure that powers our research and development — from the engineering, product design, and testing of our compute chips, networking products, and systems, to the development of our open models, such as NVIDIA Nemotron™, NVIDIA Cosmos™, and GR00T, and our autonomous vehicle software. Our upcoming data center leases have terms up to twenty years and have not commenced. These leases are expected to commence between the fourth quarter of fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2033. We may assign some of these leases to third parties in the future. Our equity investments are focused on frontier labs, infrastructure financiers, and other private companies, subject to certain contingencies. In response, we have entered into arrangements that help select customers secure the land, power and data center capacity needed to support their growth. In August 2026, we entered into guarantees to provide credit support on the land, power, and shell buildout to secure approximately 4.25 gigawatts (GW) across nine buildings at SB Energy's PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Ohio, which will exclusively host NVIDIA infrastructure under 20-year leases to OpenAI, subject to limited exceptions. Our guarantees take effect phase by phase, with each one becoming effective only when its related building is completed and ready for service. The first is expected in fiscal 2029. Our exposure therefore builds over time as each building comes online, up to a maximum of $105 billion, and then declines over each 20-year lease term as OpenAI makes lease payments. We also have the option to provide credit support in phases for approximately 3.8 additional GW as the site scales. To date, we have not made any payments related to our guarantees. We repaid $1.0 billion of senior notes in the third quarter. We are not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in our outlook. • GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be between 71.0% and 72.0%. • GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $11.0 billion and $10.7 billion, respectively.
