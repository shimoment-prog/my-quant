San Jose, Calif., November 2, 2026 – L umentum Holdings Inc. (“Lumentum” or the “Company”) today reported results for its fiscal first quarter ended September 26, 2026. “Lumentum built on its strong momentum in the first fiscal quarter of 2027, delivering growth across all our businesses, with pump lasers and optical circuit switches (OCS) being the leading contributors. During the quarter, we secured our second major NPO/CPO win, backed by purchase commitments exceeding $5 billion. Additionally, our OCS demand visibility has expanded significantly in recent months, positioning optical switching to become a multi-billion-dollar annual revenue stream. Finally, we continue to see strength in our cloud modules business which is showing improvement on the gross margin line,” said President and CEO Michael Hurlston. "Looking ahead, we anticipate adding approximately $350 million in sequential growth in our second quarter, a figure greater than the quarterly revenue of the whole company just two years ago. Furthermore, we project our second quarter operating margin to exceed 45 percent.” Fiscal First Quarter Highlights: Net revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 was $1.26 billion, with GAAP net income of $360.3 million, or $3.54 per diluted share. Net revenue for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $1.01 billion, with GAAP net loss of $7.2 billion, or $84.65 per diluted share. Net revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $533.8 million, with GAAP net income of $4.2 million, or $0.05 per diluted share. Non-GAAP net income for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 was $461.1 million, or $4.57 per diluted share. Non-GAAP net income for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $326.3 million, or $3.23 per diluted share. Non-GAAP net income for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $86.4 million, or $1.10 per diluted share. The Company held $2.0 billion in total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments at the end of the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, a decrease of $758.8 million from the end of the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026. About Lumentum Lumentum (NASDAQ: LITE) is a global leader in optical and photonic technologies that power the networks and infrastructure behind AI, cloud computing, and next-generation communications. Built on decades of photonics innovation, Lumentum delivers high-performance lasers, modules, and optical subsystems that enable scalable, energy-efficient data center connectivity, advanced telecom networks, industrial manufacturing, and sensing applications. Headquartered in San Jose, California, the company operates R&D, manufacturing, and sales facilities worldwide. Learn more at www.lumentum.com . We calculate basic net income per share pursuant to the two-class method as a result of the issuance of the Series A Convertible Preferred Stock (the “Preferred Stock”) in March 2026. Our Preferred Stock represents a second class of common stock for purposes of computing net income per share under the two-class method as it is entitled to receive dividends on an as-converted basis in the same manner as holders of common stock and does not have any material preferential rights relative to our common stock. As the Preferred Stock participates on an if-converted basis, and there are no dividends, the income allocated to the two classes of stock converge and the results are mathematically equal. Thus, basic and diluted net income per share is calculated assuming the Preferred Stock have been converted into common stock, and the related shares are included in the weighted average shares outstanding.
