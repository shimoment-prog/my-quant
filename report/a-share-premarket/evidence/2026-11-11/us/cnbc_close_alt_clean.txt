Angela Weiss | Afp | Getty Images Stock futures rose slightly on Tuesday night following a losing session in which Nvidia, coupled with hotter-than-expected inflation data, pressured the broader market. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 88 points, or 0.2%. S&P 500 futures climbed 0.2%, while Nasdaq-100 futures advanced 0.4%. In Asia, Japan's Nikkei 225 inched 0.1% higher on Wednesday. South Korea's Kospi lost 0.22%, while Australia's S&P/ASX 200 declined 0.12%. The Dow pulled back more than 150 points in regular trading, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite shed 0.4% and 0.7%, respectively. At the center of the decline was Nvidia . The chipmaker pulled back 5.5%, as CEO Jensen Huang's affair with an alleged Chinese spy weighed on the stock for the second consecutive session. Nvidia's two-day slide pulled the company's market cap down to around $4.07 trillion, its lowest since late March. Equities also fell on Tuesday after the latest U.S. inflation data solidified expectations for a December Federal Reserve rate hike. The consumer price index increased by 0.4% month over month in October , topping expectations. Expectations for a quarter-point rate increase next month rose to about 90% following the release, per the CME Group's FedWatch tool. "Despite some technical uncertainty, higher than desired levels of inflation should keep the Fed in a stance of dissatisfaction with the degree of progress on inflation remaining lackluster, so hiking the Fed Funds policy rate in December is our and the market's base case," wrote Rick Rieder, CIO of global fixed income at BlackRock. "The question now is more of one of the extent of the hike." Comments from Fed Governor Christopher Waller also led traders to price in greater chances of a quarter-point move. Traders on Wednesday will keep their eyes on the artificial intelligence trade, after CoreWeave reported third-quarter revenue that beat analyst expectations. The report initially sent the neocloud stock slightly higher. The U.S. bond market will be closed on Wednesday for Veterans Day. 1 Hour Ago Asia-Pacific markets open subdued Asia-Pacific markets opened mixed on Wednesday. Japan's Nikkei 225 inched 0.1% higher, while the Topix was up 0.65%. South Korea's Kospi lost 0.22%, and the small-cap Kosdaq declined 2.1%. Australia's S&P/ASX 200 declined 0.12%. — Lee Ying Shan 2 Hours Ago Asia-Pacific markets set to open lower after Wall Street falls Asia-Pacific markets were set to open lower Wednesday, tracking Wall Street losses overnight as hotter-than-expected U.S. inflation data fuels fears of Federal Reserve rate hikes, while a sell-off in Nvidia shares weighed on technology stocks.
