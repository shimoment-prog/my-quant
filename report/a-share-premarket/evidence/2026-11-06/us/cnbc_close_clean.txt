Level 1 and Level 2 Market-Wide Circuit Breakers that halt trading for a minimum of 15 minutes only come into effect between 9:30 am ET and 3:25 pm ET if the S&P 500 drops by 7% or more (Level 1) and 13% (Level 2). A Level 3 decline of 20% or more can come into effect at any time and halts trading for the rest of the day. When were the circuit breakers last used? Four times in March 2020 , twice before the major Covid lockdowns began to be put in to place on March 15, and twice after.
